# Data provenance

The analysis uses rvest to collect numerical observations and policy records from the U.S. Treasury, Federal Reserve, and Bureau of Labor Statistics.

## Extraction chain

1. `read_html()` retrieves and parses each public document. Saved snapshots reduce repeated requests and make later runs repeatable.
2. `html_table()` extracts recent Treasury yields, Federal Reserve policy-history tables, CPI, and unemployment. `html_elements()`, `html_element()`, and `html_text2()` extract the older Treasury XML fields and announcement text.
3. Calendar links connect each policy change to its official statement. The printed date and decision direction are checked against the policy-history record.
4. CPI inflation is calculated as the 12-month percentage change in the seasonally adjusted CPI-U index.
5. Announcement-day Treasury changes subtract the previous Treasury observation and convert percentage-point changes into basis points.

## Snapshots and checks

`data/raw/` holds the parsed source documents. `yield_source`, `policy_source`, and `statement_source` identify source snapshots, while `statement_url` and `decision_evidence` preserve the supporting policy record. `results/source_log.csv` maps 26 documents to URLs, formats, snapshot paths, and SHA256 fingerprints. `results/output_manifest.csv` fingerprints the generated files.

Validation covers dates, duplicate observations, numeric conversion, missing values, policy-direction agreement, event joins, snapshot fingerprints, and output fingerprints. The completed run contains 1,178 distinct Treasury dates, 3,534 yield values, 56 monthly macro dates, and 18 unique rate-change announcements. All three figures and their supporting tables are generated by the R workflow.
